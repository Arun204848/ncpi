Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions :-

Can you raise a complaint ?

You can submit your complaint details online without visiting an office in person.

Is there any time limit ?

Consumer complaints generally have a limitation period, commonly cited as around two years from the cause of action — check with our team for how this applies to your specific case.

How can consumer contact you ?

Fill out the complaint form on this page, and our team will contact you to discuss next steps.

What if I'm not satisfied with a Court decision ?

We can discuss the options available to you, including any applicable appeal processes, based on your specific matter.

Can a complaint be resolved fully online ?

Some cases can be progressed largely online, but this depends on the nature of the complaint — not every case is suited to a fully online process.
